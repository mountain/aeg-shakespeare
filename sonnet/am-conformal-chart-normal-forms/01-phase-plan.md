# AM Conformal Charts and Joint Normal Forms: Phase Plan

This file turns the problem contract in
[`00-problem-frontier.md`](00-problem-frontier.md) into staged gates.  Later
phases must not manufacture success by changing the task, grammar, oracle, or
cost ruler after seeing the result.

## Phase 0 — Freeze objects, equivalence, and cost

### Goal

Establish the first executable contract without yet searching for new physical
structure.

### Required frozen data

- **Task:** inputs, outputs, admissible error, and physical-time or event
  semantics;
- **atlas:** chart domains, overlaps, singularities, and marked points;
- **transformation classes:** record conformal charts, Möbius transformations,
  gauge transformations, covers, time reparameterizations, and task quotients
  separately;
- **candidate grammar:** generators, composition depth, coefficient height,
  local domains, and deduplication rules;
- **units:** the transport law for the $(0,1,\infty)$ frame and physical units;
- **modules:** the polynomial-like candidate syntax, matrix-like bases, and
  admissible gauges;
- **cost:** eight axes for symbols, actions, singularities, atlas, decoder,
  units, evaluation, and residuals;
- **oracle:** which classical objects may be used only for verification and
  which may be supplied openly as baseline inputs.

### First certificate

For every candidate $\phi$, verify automatically that

$$
\phi^{-1}\circ\phi=\operatorname{id},
\qquad
\pi\circ\phi^{-1}\circ\phi=\pi
$$

on the declared domain, and record its derivative, conformal factor, unit
image, singularity images, and chart-switching conditions.

### Gate 0

Proceed to Phase 1 only if at least one nontrivial candidate passes exact
round-trip, readout, unit, and action-transport certificates and its cost can
be recomputed stably.  Otherwise the Sonnet stops at the definition audit.

## Phase 1 — Riccati/projective positive control and neighboring no-go

### 1A. Exact positive control

Starting from

$$
a=-x/y,
\qquad
\dot a=c_0+c_1a+c_2a^2,
$$

derive the two-dimensional matrix lift independently and verify matrix
conjugation/connection transport under every admissible Möbius chart.  Check
explicitly that the scalar gauge $L\mapsto L+\gamma I$ leaves the projective
readout unchanged.

### 1B. Discovery test

Do not give the target matrix to the proposal generator.  Supply only the
Riccati coefficients, primitive AM actions, projective readout, and frozen
grammar.  Record whether it recovers a task-equivalent sparse two-component
presentation within the budget.

### 1C. Neighboring red team

Add a generic cubic scalar field and test whether the algorithm incorrectly
claims the same two-dimensional linear lift.  If the field can be handled only
by increasing dimension or by adding a cover or nonconformal transformation,
record that addition explicitly.

### Gate 1

- exact symbolic certificates pass;
- the discoverer and hand-supplied oracle remain strictly isolated;
- the Riccati positive control and cubic red team are both classified
  correctly; and
- joint cost, rather than matrix-entry count, is reported.

Phase 1 may claim at most a **level-1 re-expression**.  If the restricted
search recovers the structure independently, bounded discovery may be reported
only on the frozen domain, without extrapolation.

## Phase 2 — Joint search for a pendulum conformal atlas and period/module

### 2A. Preserve the first-principles entry

Start from the existing Cartesian pendulum primitive quantities, constraints,
and task readout, and recheck the derivation of

$$
Y^2=2(E-U)(1-U^2),
\qquad
dt=dU/Y.
$$

This carrier is the baseline, not a discovery of this Sonnet.

### 2B. Freeze task families

At minimum, keep the following tasks separate:

- small-amplitude behavior near an equilibrium;
- period/orbit evaluation for ordinary bounded oscillations;
- the rotation regime; and
- long-clock and degeneration tests near the separatrix.

For each family, declare the energy domain, accuracy, query workload, and
allowed number of charts independently.

### 2C. Native chart search

The proposal generator receives only task-visible marks, the AM grammar, and
cost rulers.  It searches over

- low-height Möbius configurations of branch and marked points;
- local charts generated by finite Addition/Multiplication actions;
- joint choices of chart and finite period/module basis; and
- atlas-switching strategies where required.

Legendre parameters, classical cross-ratio pairings, the $j$-invariant, period
ratios, and named elliptic functions initially remain on the oracle side.

### 2D. Exact calibration and numerical pressure

- Perform exact calibration at $E=0$ without giving $\tau=i$ to the proposal
  generator.
- In ordinary energy regimes, compare symbolic length, action sparsity,
  decoder cost, and numerical conditioning.
- Approach the equilibrium and separatrix from both directions and record
  chart failure, period degeneration, and switching cost.
- Verify reconstruction of physical time, not only the geometric orbit.

### Gate 2

Proceed only if all of the following hold:

1. exact commuting certificates cover chart, carrier, module, unit, clock, and
   decoder;
2. a strict Pareto improvement occurs on at least one workload frozen in
   advance;
3. failure domains and atlas switches are not hidden in degenerate regimes;
4. an independent classical oracle can verify the discovered result; and
5. the report identifies the modulus/period/monodromy data that no chart can
   remove.

If the only result is a correct restatement of a classical normalization, close
the phase with a level-1 conclusion.

## Phase 3 — PCR3BP local-task stress test

### 3A. Select tasks rather than mixing them

Commission the following tasks one at a time rather than claiming to cover all
of them at once:

- state propagation over a fixed time window;
- Poincaré return on a declared section;
- a local segment near one primary but away from collision; and
- a regularized segment including the collision limit.

For each task, freeze the mass parameter, Jacobi-constant interval, initial-data
domain, tolerance, stopping conditions, and baseline.

### 3B. Transformation audit

The candidate pipeline must treat

1. a conformal chart;
2. a ramified cover;
3. time reparameterization; and
4. basis gauge for the variational equation/monodromy

as four separate switches in an ablation.  Attribute every gain to its actual
source.

### 3C. Joint cost

Compare the local polynomial-like coefficient description with the matrix-like
variation/return action table while also charging for the atlas, event
location, physical-time reconstruction, condition number, and residual.

Exchange only certified task/cost interfaces with
[`../pcr3bp-history-cost/`](../pcr3bp-history-cost/).  Do not borrow its
Bellman/Huffman account before that account has passed its own Phase 2.

### Gate 3

- At least one clearly defined local task shows a reproducible net improvement
  over standard coordinates/standard regularization.
- Independent integration and conservation-law/return-residual checks pass.
- Collision, branch, and chart-boundary failure modes are triggered
  explicitly.
- No claim exceeds the evidence by asserting global integrability or a unique
  optimal chart.

A negative result is equally valid: it may show that the native AM chart
grammar offers no benefit beyond classical local normalization.

## Phase 4 — Abstraction extraction candidate

Only if at least two independent problems in Phases 1--3 force the same
interface may the study propose a research-local abstraction:

$$
\text{task}
\longrightarrow
(\text{conformal atlas},\text{carrier},\text{module},\text{decoder},\text{cost})
\longrightarrow
\text{certified Pareto frontier}.
$$

### Gate 4

- **Precision gate:** definitions, domains, equivalence, and failure conditions
  are unambiguous.
- **Calibration gate:** at least two independent prototypes have
  exact/numerical evidence.
- **Abstraction gate:** the abstraction removes duplication and predicts new
  tests rather than merely renaming existing material.
- **Foundation gate:** it can be translated back into histories, tasks, lifts,
  quotients, observers, and units.

Even after these gates pass, the result can only become an extraction candidate
and then mature through `experimental/`.  Direct promotion from a Sonnet to the
Public API is prohibited.

## Suggested first deliverables

Before Phase 0/1 is complete, keep the deliverables small:

- `chart_contract.py`: declared domains, Möbius/local conformal candidates,
  round trips, and unit transport;
- `riccati_projective_certificate.py`: the exact two-dimensional lift, gauge,
  and cubic red team;
- `cost_schema.py`: the eight-axis cost and Pareto dominance; and
- small exact tests containing only rational/symbolic examples and no
  expensive search.

These filenames are plans only and are not created by the initialization
commit.
