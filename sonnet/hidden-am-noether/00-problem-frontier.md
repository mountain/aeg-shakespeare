# Phase 0 — problem frontier and frozen search contract

**Status:** open Sonnet problem; bounded Phase-0 contract; answer unknown.

## 1. Problem-native statement

Let arithmetic process histories be generated by two Addition/Multiplication
pairs

\[
(A_x,M_x),\qquad(A_y,M_y),
\]

with

\[
[A_x,M_x]=A_x,
\qquad
[A_y,M_y]=A_y,
\]

and cross-pair commutators zero.  Begin from an expression in a finite grammar
over state/process atoms, not from a known Lagrangian coordinate system or
Noether charge.

The first open question is:

> Within the frozen grammar, does there exist an expression with no visible
> generator symmetry for which a bounded A/M observer history exposes an exact
> nonzero continuous symmetry?  If yes, what is the minimum presentation cost?

The admissible outputs are either:

1. a minimal positive witness with exact residual certificates; or
2. an exhaustive negative certificate for the frozen bound.

## 2. Primitive audit

### Primitive process data

- literal state atoms: `x`, `y`;
- first process-jet atoms: `vx`, `vy`;
- nonzero small integer constants: `-2`, `-1`, `1`, `2`;
- expression constructors: commutative `Add` and `Mul`;
- process generators: `A_x`, `M_x`, `A_y`, `M_y`;
- literal observer histories: words in those four generators.

The initial grammar deliberately excludes square root, exponential, logarithm,
trigonometric functions, Fourier modes, matrices, and arbitrary coordinate
maps.  Later enlargement requires a new frozen census rather than silent
mutation of the frontier.

### Task semantics

For a raw expression (F), a visible generator candidate (X), and an observer
history (g), the S2 target is an exact certificate of the form

\[
X(F)\ne0,
\qquad
Y(F)\ne0\quad\text{for every visible candidate }Y,
\]

but

\[
X_g(g\cdot F)=0
\]

for a nonzero generator (X_g) discovered without importing the hidden observer
or a classical symmetry oracle.  The precise finite/pullback semantics of
(g\cdot F) are a Phase-1 implementation obligation, not assumed here.

## 3. Frozen Phase-0 bounds

```text
expression atoms       x, y, vx, vy, -2, -1, 1, 2
expression operations  Add, Mul
expression depth       <= 2 (Phase 1 may begin at <= 1 for census validation)
generator coefficients {-1, 0, 1}, modulo overall sign
observer alphabet      A_x, M_x, A_y, M_y
observer word length   <= 3
certificate arithmetic exact symbolic identities only
```

At depth zero there are eight expressions.  At depth one, retaining literal
commutative syntax before semantic quotienting, there are 72 binary nodes and
80 cumulative candidates.  The projectivized nonzero generator grammar has
40 candidates; literal observer histories through length three have 85 words,
including the empty history.

These counts are executable invariants in `phase0_contract.py`.  Phase 1 must
record both raw counts and exact semantic quotient counts.

## 4. Oracle firewall

The discovery run may not receive:

- a hidden coordinate or observer transformation;
- a classical Noether charge or momentum formula;
- a preselected cyclic coordinate;
- a Lagrangian known to become symmetric after a named transformation;
- the output of a general Lie-symmetry solver;
- numerical labels produced from the intended hidden answer;
- a frontier witness found in an unrestricted pilot search.

Traditional calculus and external solvers may be used only after a candidate is
frozen, as independent verification and comparison.

The executable contract keeps `discovery_inputs` and `hidden_oracles` disjoint.
This is not a security mechanism; it is an auditable research-discipline gate.

## 5. Exact certificate obligations

A positive S2 witness must include:

1. the literal raw expression and its grammar derivation;
2. the complete lower-cost census;
3. exact nonzero residuals for every visible generator candidate;
4. the discovered observer word and generator;
5. an exact zero residual after transport/canonicalization;
6. a perturbation red team that destroys the claimed symmetry;
7. reconstruction data showing the observer did not change task semantics;
8. a classical post-hoc verification that was not available to discovery.

A negative result must include the frozen grammar hash/bounds, raw and quotient
candidate counts, every pruning rule with a soundness certificate, and the
empty surviving witness set.

## 6. Staged claims

```text
S1  exact visible A/M symmetry
S2  symmetry visible only after discovered canonical observer
S3  symmetry produces a nonzero boundary/history payload
S4  payload depends on path, not only endpoints
S5  a closed history has nontrivial holonomy
S6  task extraction changes an optimum or yields a new complexity result
```

No later label may be inferred from an earlier one.  In particular, symmetry
does not imply an ordered cost, and holonomy does not imply Bellman
scalarization.

## 7. Kill conditions

The current formulation must be stopped or revised if:

1. every apparent hidden witness is introduced by the declared observer
   grammar rather than forced by task equivalence;
2. exact semantic quotienting collapses the search to ordinary coordinate
   symmetry;
3. the first-process-jet atoms already import the traditional tangent ontology
   in a way the A/M history cannot reconstruct;
4. the observer word has no independently checkable reconstruction semantics;
5. search ranking requires the hidden classical answer;
6. the bounded question grows faster than its exact negative certificates can
   be audited.

## 8. Relation to Process Geometry

The Sonnet tests a missing arrow rather than assuming it:

\[
\text{raw A/M process}
\dashrightarrow
\text{canonical observer discovery}
\dashrightarrow
\text{Noether/history payload}.
\]

Success would support this arrow.  Failure may split it into separate
structures or show that a second-order A/M jet is required before observer
discovery is meaningful.  No Theory Map change is made at Phase 0.
