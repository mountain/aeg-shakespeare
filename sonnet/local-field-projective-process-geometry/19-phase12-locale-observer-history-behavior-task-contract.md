## 0. Initialization decision

The revised line is **variance and semantic fibration first, duality only when
earned, place second**.

Here *semantic fibration* is provisional language for a typed map from a
detailed process carrier to a coarser task carrier together with its fibres,
residuals, and any declared transport or conditional laws.  It does not assert
a Hurewicz or Serre fibration.

The revision does not discard ordinary real or p-adic topology, frame/locale
duality, or full coalgebraic behavior.  It changes their logical position:

~~~text
not the foundation                         retained research role
---------------------------------------    ------------------------------------
p-adic topology as "the dual space"        non-Archimedean observer instance
continued fraction as mother object        marked section / symbolic coding
full behavior equality at every layer      maximal exact temporal control
bidual return as objectification gate       strongest recovery grade only
single-valued rank lowering                 strict special case of fibre lowering
residual as automatic new dimension         vertical datum or candidate only
~~~

The phase will first ask which forward process maps, backward observation
maps, cross-layer semantic adapters, and fibre transports are forced by the
declared tasks.  It may then determine whether any adjunction or dual
equivalence is actually earned.

The following boundaries remain frozen:

- real and p-adic topologies are different place-indexed realizations;
- exact quotients and full behaviors remain mandatory positive controls;
- forgetting must be typed by fibres, residuals, horizons, and error budgets;
- a fibre, ensemble, or repaired observer is not automatically a new rank;
- no result, executable, theorem promotion, or API proposal is authorized.

## 1. Central question

The phase now asks:

> Can the A/M route be organized around task-relative semantic fibrations in
> which spatial observations transport contravariantly, temporal processes
> admit coherent behaviour adaptation and fibre transport, and objectified
> primitives remain compositionally grounded even when inter-layer passages
> intentionally forget information?

Real and p-adic topologies remain place-indexed realizations of the observer
interface rather than mutual duals.

The question has six logically separate parts.

1. **Spatial observer question.**  Does inverse image on open predicates give
   the correct extension from finite Boolean powersets to nontrivial
   topologies?
2. **Temporal observer question.**  What is the relation among literal free
   histories, full Moore behaviours, and task-selected or finite-horizon
   behaviour presentations?
3. **Adaptation question.**  When source and target layers have different
   states or continuation interfaces, which adapter data make their
   observations exactly, approximately, or only interpretively compatible?
4. **Fibre question.**  Which forgotten distinctions live in exact quotient
   fibres, adapter source fibres, conditional-probability fibres, filtered
   fibres, or reconstruction residuals?
5. **Closure question.**  When does process evolution descend to the base, when
   does it require a fibre transport, and when is a section, kernel, jet, or
   retained history required to obtain autonomous target dynamics?
6. **Objectification question.**  Can a stable base interface support new free
   composition together with coherent realization into lower fibres for every
   legal composite, with relation soundness, error propagation, and cost made
   explicit?

The phase must permit mixed verdicts.  Locale observation may succeed while
temporal adaptation remains only task-local.  Exact quotient descent may fail
while a stochastic or asymptotic fibre transport succeeds.  None of these
outcomes may borrow the name of duality or objectification from another.

## 2. Why Phase 11 is not yet the required infinite framework

Phase 11 used, for a finite set \(X\),

\[
\operatorname{Pred}(X)=\mathcal P(X)
\]

as a Boolean algebra.  This is exact on finite discrete spaces.  It does not
extend unchanged to the real line.

For a connected space such as \(\mathbb R\), the clopen Boolean algebra is

\[
\operatorname{Clop}(\mathbb R)=\{\varnothing,\mathbb R\}.
\]

It cannot distinguish the points or ordinary interval neighborhoods of the
real line.  Therefore the finite Stone model cannot be declared the universal
observer logic for the real completion.

The first candidate correction is to retain **all open predicates**.  For a
space \(X\), write

\[
\Omega(X)=\mathcal O(X).
\]

The opens form a frame: a complete lattice in which finite meets distribute
over arbitrary joins.  A continuous map \(f:X\to Y\) induces

\[
f^{-1}:\Omega(Y)\longrightarrow\Omega(X),
\]

preserving finite meets and arbitrary joins.

This retains the exact variance discovered in Phase 11 while allowing
connected spaces to have a rich observation logic.

The correction is only a candidate foundation.  The phase must still test:

- whether the relevant observer predicates are genuinely open;
- whether all opens are computationally usable or need a basis presentation;
- whether points can be recovered from the frame;
- which sobriety or spatiality hypotheses are required;
- whether process actions are continuous for the induced topology;
- whether the locale construction couples correctly to histories and tasks.

## 4. Candidate mother diagram

The phase will not assume one dual equivalence.  Its candidate mother
organization has a vertical semantic map and two differently typed horizontal
transports:

\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
E & \xrightarrow{\delta_u} & E'\\
\downarrow\pi && \downarrow\pi'\\
B & \xrightarrow{\bar\delta_u} & B'
\end{array}
\qquad
\Omega(B')\xrightarrow{\bar\delta_u^{-1}}\Omega(B).
\]

Here \(E\) is a detailed carrier, \(B\) is a task-relative semantic base, and

\[
F_b=\pi^{-1}(b)
\]

is the forgotten or conditionally retained fibre over \(b\).  The lower square
may commute exactly, commute only after declared observations, commute within
an error budget, or fail until the base is enriched by a jet, history, or
closure datum.

A high-level process \(u:b\to b'\) may therefore have one of several lower
realizations:

\[
K_u:F_b\rightsquigarrow F_{b'},
\]

where \(K_u\) can be a function, relation, stochastic kernel, or
resource-indexed approximate transport.  A chosen section \(s:B\to E\) and a
conditional law \(\nu_b\) on \(F_b\) are separate data; neither is supplied by
the existence of \(\pi\).

The process-local candidate datum is provisionally

\[
(E,B,\pi,\Sigma,\delta,\mathcal L,o,
  \kappa,\eta,\varepsilon,K,\nu,s),
\]

with only the fields justified by a particular problem retained.  In
particular:

- \(\kappa\) translates target continuations to admissible source
  continuations;
- \(\eta\) translates results;
- \(\varepsilon\) records task, topology, horizon, and error budget;
- \(K\) records fibre transport or realization;
- \(\nu\) records conditional fibre laws when probability is present;
- \(s\) records an optional section or closure choice.

This is a research ledger, not a proposed ProcessObserver or
SemanticFibration class.

## 5. Spatial candidate — frames and locales

### 5.1 Frame

A frame \(L\) is a complete lattice with

\[
a\wedge\bigvee_i b_i
=
\bigvee_i(a\wedge b_i).
\]

A frame homomorphism preserves finite meets and arbitrary joins.  Write

\[
\mathbf{Frm}
\]

for the category of frames and frame homomorphisms.

### 5.2 Locale

Freeze the classical candidate definition

\[
\mathbf{Loc}=\mathbf{Frm}^{op}.
\]

For a topological space \(X\), the frame \(\Omega(X)\) is its locale of open
observations.  A continuous state map gives a locale arrow through inverse
image.

The phase must not claim that every frame is the open-set frame of an
ordinary point-set space.  A locale may be non-spatial.

### 5.3 Points and return

A candidate point of a locale with frame \(L\) is a frame homomorphism

\[
p:L\to 2,
\]

equivalently a completely prime filter.  For a topological point \(x\in X\),

\[
p_x(U)=1\iff x\in U.
\]

The phase must distinguish:

```text
T0 separation      different points have different open profiles
sobriety            irreducible closed data has a unique generic point
spatial frame       enough points recover distinct frame elements
point recovery      X -> Pt(Omega(X)) is bijective only under hypotheses
frame recovery      L -> Omega(Pt(L)) is an isomorphism only under hypotheses
```

Any unit/counit or bidual-return claim must state the two categories, arrow
directions, and the hypotheses under which the comparison maps are
isomorphisms.  “Apply duality twice and return” is not an admissible untyped
argument.

### 5.4 Finite positive controls

The future executable should use two finite controls.

1. For a finite discrete set \(X\), verify that
   \(\Omega(X)=\mathcal P(X)\), recovering the Phase 11 Boolean case.
2. For the Sierpinski space with opens
   \(\varnothing,\{1\},\{0,1\}\), verify a non-Boolean frame, enumerate its
   points, and test inverse image for all continuous self-maps.

These controls test the extension from Boolean predicates to open predicates.
They do not prove a representation theorem for arbitrary locales.

### 5.5 Infinite theorem controls

The future result must treat the following as theorem-level controls, not as
facts proved by bounded enumeration:

- the open frame of \(\mathbb R\) is rich while its clopen algebra is trivial;
- sober spaces correspond to spatial locales under the appropriate classical
  equivalence;
- the real line and p-adic fields satisfy different separation,
  connectedness, and basis properties;
- non-spatial locales and non-sober spaces prevent an unrestricted pointwise
  round trip.

An infinite set with the cofinite topology is a mandatory sobriety red team:
the whole space is irreducible closed but, in the \(T_1\) case, is not the
closure of a unique point.

### 5.6 Computational presentation of opens

The frame of all opens can be too large or noncomputable to materialize.
Therefore the future solver must begin with a declared basis or frame
presentation:

```text
basis generators
finite-meet relations
declared cover/join relations
normalization or equality certificate
point/satisfaction evaluator
resource budget
```

A basis is not itself automatically a frame.  The generated frame and the
relations identifying unions must be stated.

## 6. Temporal candidate — free histories and response behaviors

### 6.1 Free histories

For a frozen process alphabet \(\Sigma\), let

\[
\Sigma^*
\]

be the free monoid of finite histories, with empty word \(\epsilon\) and
concatenation.  The phase must explicitly prove or invoke the free-monoid
universal property for the declared category; the word *free* may not be
inferred from the use of a list data structure.

For a deterministic action, fix a convention

\[
\delta^*(x,\epsilon)=x,
\qquad
\delta^*(x,aw)=\delta^*(\delta(x,a),w).
\]

Changing to right-appended chronology requires a declared opposite
convention and corresponding changes to every derivative formula.

### 6.2 Response behaviors

For an output set \(\Omega\) and terminal observation \(o:X\to\Omega\), define

\[
\beta_x(w)=o(\delta^*(x,w)).
\]

The full behavior space candidate is

\[
\Omega^{\Sigma^*}.
\]

For \(a\in\Sigma\), define the left derivative

\[
(\partial_a\beta)(w)=\beta(aw).
\]

The Moore-coalgebra candidate is

\[
c:X\longrightarrow\Omega\times X^\Sigma,
\qquad
c(x)=\bigl(o(x),(\delta(x,a))_{a\in\Sigma}\bigr).
\]

Classically, \(\Omega^{\Sigma^*}\) with head evaluation and word derivatives
is a final coalgebra for the functor

\[
F(Z)=\Omega\times Z^\Sigma
\]

on sets.  Phase 12 must type and verify this result for the chosen convention;
it must not infer a categorical duality between the free monoid and the final
coalgebra merely because one is free and the other is final.

### 6.3 Myhill--Nerode connection

Behavioral equivalence is

\[
x\sim_o y
\iff
\forall w\in\Sigma^*:\ \beta_x(w)=\beta_y(w).
\]

For Boolean output, each state behavior is a language.  The image of

\[
\beta:X\to\Omega^{\Sigma^*}
\]

is the task-visible behavior carrier.  A finite-state quotient should agree
with the coarsest stable output-preserving partition.

The future executable must use exact coinductive or partition-refinement
certificates.  A bounded table through depth \(N\) is insufficient: a
mandatory counterexample must agree through depth \(N\) and separate at
depth \(N+1\).


### 6.4 Task behaviour projections and fibres

The full behaviour map is a maximal exact control, not the compulsory target
of every semantic layer.  For a declared task family, horizon, and resolution,
write schematically

\[
R_{\mathcal Q,T,\varepsilon}:
\Omega^{\Sigma^*}\longrightarrow B_{\mathcal Q,T,\varepsilon},
\qquad
\beta^{\mathcal Q,T,\varepsilon}_x
=R_{\mathcal Q,T,\varepsilon}(\beta_x).
\]

Its fibres collect states whose distinctions are invisible to that adapter.
They need not be equivalence classes when the comparison is approximate.

The derivative action may:

1. descend exactly to the task base;
2. induce a relation or set-valued successor among fibres;
3. induce a stochastic kernel relative to conditional fibre laws;
4. require a jet, retained history, or closure section;
5. fail to be adequate under the declared horizon.

The finite Boltzmann--BBGKY witness is a mandatory control: equal present
marginals can have unequal next derivatives.  Adding the first process jet
repairs that task without reconstructing the complete source state or future.

### 6.5 What must be audited

The temporal side has five possible outcomes that must not be conflated:

1. **evaluation only:** histories act and produce outputs;
2. **coalgebraic semantics:** every state has a unique behavior map into a
   declared final coalgebra;
3. **semantic fibration:** a task base, source fibres, and coherent transport
   or an explicit nonclosure boundary are typed;
4. **adjunction:** specified functors admit a unit and counit with triangle
   identities;
5. **dual equivalence:** the adjunction restricts to mutually recoverable
   subcategories.

Phase 12 earns only the strongest level whose types, identities, adaptation
grades, and return conditions are proved.

## 7. Coupling spatial and temporal observation

For one state space \(X\), an open predicate \(U\in\Omega(X)\), and a history
\(w\in\Sigma^*\), the strict within-layer control is

\[
\delta^*(x,w)\in U
\iff
x\in(\delta_w)^{-1}(U).
\]

This exact equality remains the central process--observer square for a
continuous action.  It couples forward temporal action to backward spatial
observation without identifying them.

Across different semantic layers, the corresponding requirement is an adapter
square.  For source state \(x\), target continuation \(k\), and task \(q\),

\[
d_q\!\left(
q_B(\pi(x)\cdot k),
\eta_q\bigl(q_E(x\cdot\kappa_x(k))\bigr)
\right)
\leq\varepsilon_q(k).
\]

The phase must record whether the bound is pointwise on each fibre, uniform on
a task domain, in probability, in expectation under \(\nu_b\), weak against a
test family, or asymptotic in a declared limit.

The correct observer carrier may be:

- the full frame \(\Omega(X)\);
- a basis-generated or task-selected subframe;
- a full or projected behaviour space;
- a sheaf, bundle, relation, or kernel over the task base;
- or another object forced by partial, stochastic, quantitative, or
  noncontinuous processes.

No generic answer is frozen.  Exact naturality is the positive control;
controlled noncommutation is admissible adaptation; unmeasured mismatch is
failure.

## 8. Quotients, fibres, residuals, and objectification

### 8.1 Exact spatial quotient control

For a surjection \(q:X\twoheadrightarrow Y\), inverse image is injective on
sets.  To identify \(\Omega(Y)\) with all fibre-saturated opens of \(X\),

\[
U\subseteq X\text{ open and fibre-saturated}
\Longleftrightarrow
U=q^{-1}(V)\text{ for some open }V\subseteq Y,
\]

the topology on \(Y\) must be the quotient topology, or an equivalent descent
condition must be proved.  A continuous surjection alone is not enough.

This remains the locale refinement of Phase 11's finite fibre-constant
predicate theorem.

### 8.2 Exact temporal quotient control

For a deterministic task observer, an autonomous quotient
\(q:X\twoheadrightarrow Y\) must preserve output and make transitions descend:

\[
o=\bar o\circ q,
\qquad
q(\delta(x,a))=\bar\delta(q(x),a).
\]

Equivalently, its kernel is an output-preserving transition congruence.  The
task-minimal exact quotient identifies equal full behaviours.

This is the strongest deterministic descent control.  It is not the only
legal cross-layer relation.

### 8.3 Semantic fibres beyond exact quotients

For a task-relative map \(\pi:E\to B\), a fibre \(F_b\) records source
distinctions forgotten at the base.  Four outcomes must be separated:

1. **strong descent:** every source point in \(F_b\) has the same target
   successor;
2. **relational descent:** successors determine a typed relation between base
   states or fibres;
3. **measured descent:** a conditional law \(\nu_b\) and transport kernel
   determine the target law;
4. **nonclosure:** target evolution is not autonomous until the carrier is
   enlarged by a jet, history, residual, section, or kernel.

A section or ensemble can repair nonclosure only for its declared task and
regime.  It must not be hidden in the chart, quotient, or object name.

### 8.4 Filtered and approximate fibres

For a family indexed by scale, horizon, or resolution, adequacy may be exact
only after the index becomes part of the carrier.  The future phase may use
relations such as

\[
x_\epsilon\sim_k y_\epsilon
\iff
d_q(q(x_\epsilon c),q(y_\epsilon c))=o(\epsilon^k)
\]

when continuation stability and the quantifiers are proved.

Approximate objectification requires an explicit composition law for error.
For example, a bound may have the schematic form

\[
\varepsilon_{v\circ u}
\leq
\varepsilon_u+L_u\varepsilon_v.
\]

If the error leaves the declared budget after repeated composition, the legal
higher grammar must be horizon- or resource-indexed.  One-step adequacy does
not earn unrestricted objectification.

### 8.5 Residual ledger

Every residual must be classified before it is interpreted vertically.

~~~text
non-saturated open / predicate       missing spatial discriminator
unequal full response                missing temporal distinction
source fibre                         deliberately forgotten source variation
conditional fibre law                ensemble-relative retained variation
place mark                           observer-selection residual
frame/cusp/unit data                 reconstruction residual
literal word above same action       chronology/provenance residual
closure or section choice            autonomy-producing extra datum
controlled adaptation defect         filtered or approximate residual
new freely composable interface      possible objectification pressure
~~~

### 8.6 Revised objectification gate

The strict conservative gate remains a positive control:

~~~text
new task-stable primitive
+ free composition producing unseen composites
+ exact all-composite lowering
+ relation soundness
+ declared cost and reconstruction boundary
~~~

The enlarged candidate gate is:

~~~text
stable task-relative base interface
+ new free or typed composition on that base
+ coherent realization or transport between lower fibres
+ identity, composition, and relation soundness at the declared grade
+ explicit residual, measure, closure, horizon, and error ledger
+ declared calculability and complexity advantage
~~~

Three grades must remain distinct:

1. **strict conservative objectification:** singleton or exact semantic
   lowering with zero task error;
2. **fibred task-exact objectification:** lowering returns a fibre, relation, or
   conditional law while declared task observations commute exactly;
3. **filtered or approximate objectification:** composition is valid only with
   a proved error law and a typed scale, horizon, or resource budget.

A locale point, open, behaviour residual, measured fibre, or repaired observer
does not pass any grade by itself.  If no coherent composite realization
exists, the construction is adaptation or compression, not a new process
rank.

## 9. Place-indexed realizations

The place comparison is now also a base-change calibration.  Real and p-adic
observer presentations may be obtained from a common marked rational carrier,
but their bases, covers, completions, fibres, and sections remain different.
A change of place may induce pullback or comparison data between semantic
fibrations; it is not thereby a logical duality or an equivalence.

### 9.1 Common source and different bases

The rational A/M/projective carrier remains upstream of completion.  The
future comparison should be typed as

```text
marked rational history/action
          |
          +--> real observer presentation
          |      basis: rational intervals / order cuts
          |      completion: ordered real topology
          |      sections: floor / regular continued fractions
          |
          +--> p-adic observer presentation
                 basis: rational p-adic balls / residue cylinders
                 completion: ultrametric topology
                 sections: declared p-adic representatives / continued fractions
```

The rational intervals and rational p-adic balls are basis presentations, not
the full frames by enumeration.

### 9.2 Retained role of p-adic topology

The p-adic topology remains valuable in three bounded roles.

1. **Calibration:** it is a genuine non-Archimedean, totally disconnected
   observer whose open-frame behavior differs sharply from the real case.
2. **Coinductive model:** its nested cylinders and inverse limits pressure the
   temporal and topological boundary of finite observations.
3. **Global place component:** its valuations participate with the real place
   in the rational product formula.

None makes it the categorical opposite of the real topology.

### 9.3 Continued fractions

Real and p-adic continued fractions are to be treated as marked symbolic
sections or codings over their observer presentations.  The phase must ask:

- which open/cylinder predicates a finite prefix recognizes;
- whether word extension matches refinement or crosses a boundary;
- which frame, cusp, orientation, and representative section are retained;
- whether a resulting behavior has an exact rational-image lowering;
- whether two selectors recognize the same task language.

The existence of a useful continued-fraction coding at both places would
support the common interface.  It would not prove that either coding is
canonical, that the topologies are dual, or that the code is the mother
object.

### 9.4 Deferred global construction

The rational product formula remains a global compatibility control.  Phase
12 initialization does not authorize construction of adeles, a restricted
product topology, or a global place solver.  Those remain a later, separate
line after the observer categories and lowering images are typed.

## 10. Unit, truth, and return audit

The question “what does unit one map to, and what maps back?” must be asked
separately for strict recovery and fibred realization.

### 10.1 Spatial side

Record:

- the arithmetic state or marked projective frame containing \(1\);
- the top frame element \(\top\), meaning the always-true open;
- a point evaluation \(p_x:L\to2\);
- the comparison \(X\to\operatorname{Pt}(\Omega(X))\);
- the comparison between a frame and the opens generated by its points;
- the semantic fibre over the image of the marked unit.

The future result must say where comparison maps are injective, surjective, or
isomorphic and where the return is only a fibre, section, or relation.

### 10.2 Temporal side

Record:

- the empty history \(\epsilon\) as the monoid unit;
- the initial output \(\beta(\epsilon)\);
- derivative or transition after one generator;
- the full behaviour map and any task projection;
- the fibre of states with the same adapted behaviour;
- any section, kernel, or retained jet used to continue from that fibre.

The empty word, arithmetic one, projective unit mark, frame top, and logical
truth value are not interchangeable.

### 10.3 Return levels

Every return must name its target and strength:

~~~text
open profile                 point under T0/sobriety hypotheses
locale                       frame semantics, possibly without enough points
full behaviour               task state up to full behavioural equivalence
adapted behaviour            source fibre at the declared task and horizon
measured base point          conditional fibre law, not one microstate
marked rational frame        projective matrix semantics
canonical semantic word      one representative, not literal chronology
literal history              only with retained provenance
~~~

A return \(b\mapsto F_b\), a section \(s(b)\), and a conditional law \(\nu_b\)
are different operations.  None is an inverse unless the required identities
are proved.

evaluation, coalgebraic semantics, semantic fibration, adjunction, and dual
equivalence.  It must then classify any objectification as strict, fibred
task-exact, filtered or approximate, or absent.

## 13. Mandatory red teams

1. **Connected real versus Boolean observation.**  The clopen algebra of
   \(\mathbb R\) is trivial although its open frame separates points.
2. **Non-sober return failure.**  Point recovery cannot be claimed for every
   topology without sobriety.
3. **Non-spatial locale.**  A frame need not be determined by ordinary points.
4. **Basis is not frame.**  Rational intervals or p-adic balls require cover
   relations and generated joins.
5. **Continuous surjection is not enough.**  Saturated-open descent requires a
   quotient topology or equivalent proof.
6. **Bounded trace is not full behaviour.**  Two systems must agree through
   depth \(N\) and separate at \(N+1\).
7. **Full behaviour is not every useful layer.**  A task adapter may be exact
   for its horizon while intentionally forgetting longer continuations.
8. **Present-state agreement is not next-derivative agreement.**  The finite
   BBGKY equal-marginal witness must force a jet or closure distinction.
9. **One-step adequacy is not compositional adequacy.**  Generator errors may
   accumulate beyond the legal budget.
10. **Fibre existence is not autonomous dynamics.**  Source points in one
    fibre may induce different base successors.
11. **Ensemble is not closure.**  A conditional law or transverse weighting
    does not alone make the reduced dynamics autonomous.
12. **Final coalgebra is not dual equivalence.**  A unique behaviour map does
    not provide inverse reconstruction.
13. **p-adic fit is not universality.**  Efficient cylinders at one place do
    not make that place the mother topology.
14. **Projective duality is not logical duality.**  Incidence transport remains
    separately typed.
15. **Observer repair is not objectification.**  A separating predicate,
    residual, or measured fibre needs new composition and coherent realization.
16. **Arithmetic one is not frame top.**  Shared notation must retain typed
    units and return levels.
17. **All opens and behaviours are not free data structures.**  Materialization
    and equality costs remain visible.
18. **Place output may not lower.**  Data outside the certified rational image
    must return failure or approximation status.

## 14. Kill conditions

The proposed route is killed or materially narrowed if any of the following
occurs.

1. A claimed duality cannot name its categories, variance, and
   structure-preserving morphisms.
2. A claimed semantic fibration cannot name its detailed carrier, base,
   fibres, task, and preserved or forgotten data.
3. Locale language merely renames imported topology instead of deriving
   observer distinctions.
4. The frame or behaviour presentation cannot preserve calculability under a
   declared resource bound.
5. Process actions fail continuity and the theory has no typed place for the
   discontinuity.
6. Point or bidual recovery depends on unstated sobriety or spatiality.
7. The temporal side proves bounded agreement while claiming full behaviour.
8. A cross-layer adapter omits its continuation translator, result translator,
   horizon, topology, or error budget.
9. Fibre transport is defined on generators but has no identity, composition,
   or relation-soundness law.
10. Composite error leaves the declared budget while the higher grammar remains
    advertised as unrestricted.
11. Autonomous base dynamics depends on an undeclared section, ensemble, jet,
    or closure kernel.
12. The common interface imports p-adic-specific data unavailable to the real
    and finite controls.
13. Continued-fraction selectors are smuggled into the primitive observer
    grammar rather than retained as marked sections.
14. A new rank has no stable base interface, new composition, coherent
    all-composite fibre realization, or cost advantage.
15. Lowering or adaptation forgets task-required frame, place, branch, measure,
    or residual data without an explicit failure result.
16. Total cost is hidden by treating an infinite frame, behaviour table,
    conditional law, or objectified primitive as free storage.

A kill condition may reject one arrow or one objectification grade without
rejecting the entire Process Geometry program.
